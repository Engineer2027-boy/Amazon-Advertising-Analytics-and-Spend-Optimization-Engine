Title : Amazon Advertising Analytics Spend Optimization Engine    
______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
Amazon Advertising Analytics & Spend Optimization Engine is an end-to-end data analytics and strategy framework built with Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib. Designed to evaluate high-volume pay-per-click (PPC) performance, the project processes 134.9M+ ad impressions and $1.48M in ad spend across 2,435 Amazon Sponsored Products campaigns. The analysis uncovers $165.9K in actionable search-term waste while identifying high-performing top-of-search placement opportunities yielding a 26.8% conversion rate. The core novelty of the project lies in a multi-lens analytical model—evaluating portfolio concentration, match-type anomalies, and impression share—that establishes a strategic framework to reallocate 14% of monthly budgets and unlock over $610K in potential sales lift.    
______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
• Built an end-to-end data pipeline using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib to analyze 134.9M+ impressions and    
1.48M dollars in ad spend across 2,435 Amazon Sponsored Products campaigns.    
• Developed an analysis report uncovering 165.9K dollars in actionable search-term waste and identifying top-of-search    
placement opportunities that yield a 26.8 percent conversion rate.    
• Formulated a novel multi-lens optimization framework spanning portfolio concentration, match-type anomalies, and    
impression share to reallocate 14 percent of monthly budget and drive 610K+ dollars in potential sales lift.
